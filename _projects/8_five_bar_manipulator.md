A five-bar parallel manipulator in which all four links are of equal length. That choice is unusual because it introduces more parallel singularities than a conventional design — so the work centred on a control strategy to pass through them. The result significantly increased the manipulator's usable workspace. The idea was implemented and validated on a physical prototype at IIT Hyderabad, with micron-level precision control.
