## Awards

**Mike Stilman Award for Outstanding Paper — Finalist**
IEEE-RAS International Conference on Humanoid Robots (Humanoids 2025), Seoul, South Korea.
One of **two finalists selected from 202 submissions**, for TARS3D.

**Best Safety Features Award** — Micron UV Robot Design Challenge, 2020.
Won against 400 contestants as the only one-person team, with the robot designed in under three days.

**Gold Medal, Autonomous Humanoid Penalty Kick** — RoboGames 2017, San Francisco.
**India's first RoboGames gold medal.** Also took Silver (humanoid freestyle), Silver (biped race), and Bronze (RC humanoid sumo) at the same event, and Bronze at RoboGames 2015.

**Third Place, Humanoid Application Challenge** — IROS 2017, Vancouver.

**Best Graduation Thesis** — EEE Department, SRM University, 2017 (among 700+ students).

**Winner, National Maker Summit** — IIT Madras, 2016, for *Aawaaz*, a bone-conduction hearing aid.

**WAT Consult Best Innovation Award** — Atmos Techfest, BITS Pilani Hyderabad, 2017.
